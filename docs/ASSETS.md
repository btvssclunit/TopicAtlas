# Asset provenance

Updated October 10, 2026 by HOD/MTL Mdm Chun Kai Xin

All new map illustrations are original built-in AI-generated artwork or original SVG. No third-party asset is distributed in the atlas. User-supplied school facade and owner's Vocab Summit sprites were visual references only, not copied assets. No school crest is reproduced.

See `20261010_radial-atlas.md` and `20261010_bvss-radial-history.md` for sources, exact prompts, version history and attribution context. The uniform guide is linked for verification, not bundled. Historical homepage snapshots reproduce this repository's own UI and original CSS; originals are traceable to commit a5b24a5.

Version 4's supplied Shan Hai Jing reference screenshot informed sparse contour linework only; no screenshot, source creature drawing or source text is distributed. The new illustration is original generated artwork. Prompts and iterations are recorded in `20261010_ink-rings-v4.md`.

### Quarter-radial studies, 2026-10-10

## Modular sprites and institutional crest, October 10 2026

`docs/mockups/assets-v16/`: original built-in imagegen transparent sprite studies, PNG exports and smaller WebPs. Exact prompts and edit provenance in `prompts.json`; selected-current flags and ground anchors in `manifest.json`. Original selected generated PNG masters in `docs/mockups/masters-v16/`. No external models, scripts or texture libraries. V16/V17 screenshot previews record the assembly evolution.

`bvss-crest.png` is the actual institutional crest, used at the owner's explicit request, not claimed as original/CC0. Source: https://www.bukitviewsec.moe.edu.sg/our-story/logo-n-song/ ; original asset https://isomer-user-content.by.gov.sg/943/38af9e75-6fdb-4c89-875f-3581678564f7/bvss-School-logo-w-no-background-high-res-300x300.png . Actual facade and attire photographs served as references, not site artwork.

The NParks photo moodboard is reference-only and links original species/photo credit pages; it is not part of the student-facing atlas. Photos remain NParks/photographers' copyright. See `docs/20261010_modular-atlas.md` and the Claude handoff for reference sources and constraints.

## Production watercolor atlas, October 30 2026

`assets/atlas/watercolor-map.webp` is the unchanged v38 generated display artwork. `title-calligraphy.webp` and `school-seal.webp` are unchanged v32 sprites; `title-vertical.webp` is the unchanged v33 sprite. Exact prompts and full masters remain in the corresponding dated handoffs and mockup archive. `bvss-crest.png` is the owner-requested institutional crest credited above. The SVG facade label is editable code. These five files total 589,534 bytes; production does not load the archived masters or reference photos. No new generation or third-party photography was used for integration.
