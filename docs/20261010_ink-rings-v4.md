## Owner direction and result

The owner requested substantially desaturated Chinese watercolour, stronger Shan Hai Jing-inspired linework, fewer elements and clearer identities for six concentric scopes. The centre pavilion also needed to become much smaller than trees and mountains.

New review candidate: `docs/mockups/20261010_ink-rings-v4.html`. The centre shelter is modest and below its adjacent tree. Sparse cues are personal garden, two family homes, school facade, HDB/hawker community, a limited Singapore silhouette and outer world water/foreign silhouettes. Fine ink outlines dominate; pale blue/sage washes and quiet paper replace dense bright decoration. Only two boats remain. The ordered controls and selected-band interaction remain, with a muted wash instead of a bright gold overlay.

Three generated studies are preserved: first desaturated redraw, structural concentric redraw, then corrected community-landmark placement. Version 3 and all earlier studies remain intact. The history gallery now contains eleven stages. Full-resolution masters remain in the local sandbox; compact exports and dated HTML live in the graphics PR. The attached reference screenshot is style guidance only, not a copied or distributed asset.

Six SVG selection bands share centre (768,515), matching the illustration approximately: (rx,ry) 145/75, 280/112, 390/190, 510/240, 610/320, 750/385.
