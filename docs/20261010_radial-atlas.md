# Radial mountains and seas: revised map mockup

Updated October 10, 2026 by HOD/MTL Mdm Chun Kai Xin

## Owner direction

The owner requested traditional 山海经-inspired illustration with a modern twist, and a radial composition expressing expanding scope from personal to world. This supersedes the earlier A/B/C proposals for the next mockup. Production integration awaits review of this concrete study.

Open `docs/mockups/TopicAtlas-radial-mockup.html` on the existing local server. Six enclosing landscapes expand from the personal garden through family courtyard houses, school, HDB/community, national waterfront and outer world sea. The perspective places the personal garden below image centre, with outward rings receding toward the skyline. Ink mountain contours, curled cloud/wave motifs, jade and teal pigment, parchment and a right-side sun establish the illustrated direction.

Names, mottos and counts are HTML text, not raster text. Buttons select an approximate band trace; these are concept contours rather than exact boundaries. The ordered scope strip provides larger keyboard/phone controls. The G3 中三 preview rings personal, family, campus and community. Theme changes flip an attribute; the dark treatment retains the parchment artwork with dark surroundings and opaque labels. No student content or production behavior changed.

## Generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: original landscape background for Topic Atlas educational map. Create a sophisticated Shan Hai Jing 山海经 inspired Chinese ink-and-mineral-pigment illustrated map with a modern editorial twist. Wide landscape 3:2 composition. CRITICAL geography: six clearly nested concentric irregular elliptical landscape bands, expanding from one tiny personal garden at exact centre through family homes, school campus, Singapore community neighbourhood, Singapore national waterfront, and finally outer world ocean. NOT six separate islands and NOT six pie slices: scope expands radially in nested enclosing bands. Centre (50% x,50% y): tiny contemplative garden with one person, book and pavilion. First enclosing band: family courtyard houses. Second band: school courtyard and small modern school buildings. Third band: HDB flats, hawker shelter, community garden. Fourth band: generic original Singapore waterfront, bridge and tropical civic skyline. Outermost band: vast curling seas, distant mountains and small sailing craft, evoking connection to the world. Chinese hand-inked contour lines, fine etched mountain ridges, stylised curled cloud and wave motifs, atmospheric cream parchment negative space, muted jade green mountains, sky blue and deep teal waters, sparse cinnabar accents and gold pigment. Three mountain peaks in distance, small warm sun upper right. Imaginative yet mature, suitable for secondary students, detailed polished illustrated game atlas, flat overhead oblique view so concentric scope is unmistakable. Leave quiet pockets at centre and along bands for live HTML labels later. NO text, letters, numbers, calligraphy, logos, watermark or UI. No textbook or copied illustrations.
