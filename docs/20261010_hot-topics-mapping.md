Updated October 10, 2026 by HOD/MTL Mdm Chun Kai Xin

Source: `BVSS_华文热门话题总表.xlsx` from the BVSS CL Resource Hub (热门话题 Hot Topics page). Extracted data: `docs/20261010_hot-topics-extract.json`. It has 77 hot topics, each with Chinese links (联合早报, 8视界, gov), English official links and 口试参考题目方向. Hot topic ids are `category.item`, following the sheet's numbering (1 教育与学习 to 10 跨域辩证框架).

Status: a mapping proposal for the owner to review. No student-facing content is changed.

## Issues to check before anything goes live

1. **Link titles vs targets.** Several Chinese titles look like descriptive labels rather than the real headline, and some point at unrelated or old articles. Examples: 5.4 维修文化 links a 2022 `lifestyle/travel` story; 6.1 and 6.2 share one AI-in-healthcare story; 1.8 and 3.4 share a 2023 story; 7.1, 7.5, 9.2, 10.1, 10.4 all use the same gov.sg explainer; 8.1, 8.3, 10.3 all use the visitsingapore home page. Each link needs opening and its `tt` set to the real headline.
2. **Dated policy.** 4.3 营养等级 and 5.1 退费制 carry "措施会更新" notes. These should go in `res[]` with a date, or in a separate file we refresh each year.
3. **Paywall and age.** 联合早报 links may hit a paywall. 早报校园 / 8视界 are the friendlier picks for students.
4. **Oral prompts are hub material**, written for teachers. Before they go in `oral.q`, each one needs leveling per stream and vetting as 待审.

## Suggested data shape (for discussion)

Hot topics are seasonal while topic content is stable, so keep them apart: a `data/hot.json` list (`id`, `name`, `topics[]`, `res[]`, `q[]`, `checked` date). A topic page then shows a 「热门话题」 strip in 资源库 or 口试会话 for its matches. A yearly refresh edits one file and leaves the vetted content files alone.
