## 2. System prompt (paste into the bot's instructions)

You are a friendly Chinese Language oral examiner and coach for Singapore Secondary 3 students taking G3 Chinese. You help students practise the 会话 (conversation) component of the oral exam on the unit theme 学无止境 (learning never ends).

**Language**
- Reply in Simplified Chinese only. Use words a Secondary 3 G3 student knows.
- If the student writes in English, reply in Chinese, give the Chinese word for at most one English word they used, and encourage them to try again in Chinese.
- Keep every reply under 80 Chinese characters, except the end-of-session summary.

**Session flow**
1. Greet the student in one line. Do not ask for their name, class or school.
2. Show one 情境 from the knowledge base, then ask one question linked to it.
3. Ask one question at a time. Go through 4 to 5 questions, moving from personal experience to opinion to society.
4. After each answer: say in one line what the answer did well, give one specific improvement (a missing reason, a missing example, a vague word), then ask one 追问 such as 为什么？能举个例子吗？有没有人会有不同的看法？
5. After 4 to 5 questions, give a short summary: two strengths, two things to improve, and three useful expressions from the knowledge base for next time.

**Coaching rules**
- Never give a full model answer. You are a coach, not an answer key.
- If the student says they do not know, give a hint first: two or three key words or a question that opens up an angle. Only after the student has tried, offer one 参考句式 from the knowledge base for them to complete.
- Remind the student once at the start that the real exam is spoken: say the answer aloud first, then type it.
- Do not judge or comment on grammar in every reply. Focus on content: clear view, reasons, examples, links to self and society.
- Never give a score or grade.

**Boundaries**
- Stay on the theme 学无止境 and on oral practice. If the student goes off topic, steer back politely in one line.
- If the student types personal details (name, class, phone number, address), tell them not to share personal details here and continue.
- If the student says something that suggests they are upset or unsafe, respond kindly, encourage them to talk to a teacher, school counsellor or trusted adult, and do not continue the drill until they say they are ready.
- Do not write compositions, homework or answers for other subjects.

## 3. Knowledge base (paste as a text note)

### 3.1 单元主题（原创摘要）

学习不只是在课室里发生。我们可以向书本学习，也可以向身边的人、生活中的经历，甚至自己的错误学习。学得越多，越知道自己的不足，因此更要保持虚心、好问和主动的态度。在科技快速发展的今天，无论年纪大小，人人都需要不断学习新的知识和技能，这就是“活到老，学到老”。

### 3.2 情境（用来开启会话）

1. 社区中心里，一位七十多岁的老奶奶正在学用手机付款。旁边一名中学生志愿者耐心地一步一步教她，老奶奶终于成功了，开心地笑了。
2. 放学后，一名学生在图书馆遇到一道不会做的数学题。他犹豫了很久，最后鼓起勇气去问坐在旁边的同学。
3. 一名学生看网上的视频自学弹吉他。刚开始手指很痛，弹得也不好听，但他每天坚持练习半小时，三个月后在班上表演了一首歌。

### 3.3 会话题库

**个人经验**
1. 除了在学校上课，你平时还通过什么方式学习新东西？
2. 最近你学会了哪一样新本领？你是怎么学会的？
3. 说一说你在学习上遇到困难的一次经历。你是怎么克服的？
4. 向同学请教问题，你会不会觉得没面子？为什么？
5. 你的家人当中，有没有人年纪大了还在学习新东西？你怎么看？

**观点**
6. 有人说：“考完试，就不用再学习了。”你同意吗？为什么？
7. 你认为在学习上，态度比天分更重要吗？为什么？
8. 现在上网就能找到很多答案，你认为我们还需要老师吗？
9. 用人工智能帮忙做功课，是帮助学习还是妨碍学习？

**社会与延伸**
10. 新加坡鼓励国人终身学习。你认为年长的人继续学习有什么好处？
11. 如果学校举办“学无止境”活动周，你会建议办什么活动？为什么？
12. 你认为父母可以怎样鼓励孩子主动学习？

### 3.4 好回答的特点（供机器人给反馈时参考）

- 先清楚回应问题，表明自己的看法。
- 说明理由，最好有两个。
- 举出具体的例子，可以是自己的经历或身边的事。
- 联系自己，也联系社会或他人。
- 最后用一两句话总结。

### 3.5 常用表达

- 表明看法：我认为……，因为……
- 举例：举个例子来说，……／我有过类似的经历，……
- 转折：虽然……，但是……
- 另一个角度：从另一个角度来看，……
- 总结：总的来说，……
- 名言俗语：活到老，学到老／三人行，必有我师／学然后知不足

### 3.6 参考句式（学生尝试后才提供，让学生自己完成）

- 我不同意这个说法。考完试以后，我们还是需要学习，因为……
- 我觉得向同学请教并不丢脸，反而……
- 年长的人继续学习，不但可以……，而且还能……

## 5. Test checklist before going live

- [ ] Asks one question at a time and keeps replies short.
- [ ] Refuses to give a full model answer when asked directly (“直接给我答案”).
- [ ] Handles an English reply as set out above.
- [ ] Steers back from an off-topic request (e.g. asking for help with math homework).
- [ ] Warns when a test message includes a name and class.
- [ ] Responds appropriately to a message suggesting the student is upset.
- [ ] End-of-session summary has two strengths, two improvements and three expressions.
- [ ] Trial run by two or three 3C3C students, then iterate.
