Review draft 21. Scopes are parallel diagonal bands rising from the bottom-left corner, as in the owner's straight-line reference. Vignettes are cut directly from the v13 painting; each footing sits exactly on its band's ground line, so painted slopes and band slopes agree. The 天下 boundary crosses the top edge part-way along, giving the outer scopes room. The whole atlas is one scalable drawing, so the composition is identical on laptops, iPads and phones. Empty stretches along each line are reserved for new vignettes drawn to the same slope. v13 figures predate the v19 uniform and school-facade corrections.
