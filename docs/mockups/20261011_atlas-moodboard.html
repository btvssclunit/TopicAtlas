Atlas · photographic reference

Actual NParks photographs for consistent botanical shapes. Original photographs remain credited to NParks and the photographers named on each species page. Reference board only; photographs are not atlas artwork.

Rain tree

Broad umbrella crown; low spreading branches; fine leaflets.

Frangipani

Open sculptural branches; thick leaves at tips; five-petal flowers.

Hibiscus

Shrub silhouette; toothed leaves; large flower and projecting staminal column.

Bougainvillea

Arching woody stems; papery coloured bracts, distinct from tiny true flowers.

Great Wall · Mutianyu winter reference

Follow the winding stone ramparts and watchtowers over uneven rocky ridges. For the distant atlas motif, simplify the bare branches and dry scrub into a few loose ink marks; omit decorative flower beds and tropical planting. The photo records a winter view, rather than vegetation in every season.
