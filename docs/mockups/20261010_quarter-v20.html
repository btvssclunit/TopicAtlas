Review draft 20. Vignettes are cut directly from the v13 painting the owner preferred and re-seated so each footing sits on a true circle around the bottom-left corner. The rings are larger than before: the boundary into 天下 crosses the top edge near its middle, so every scope has room to breathe. Because the whole atlas is a single scalable drawing, the composition is identical on laptops, iPads and phones. Gaps in 国家 and 天下 are deliberately left for later vignettes (Merlion, Supertrees, shophouses, New Seven Wonders) once they are redrawn in v13 style. v13 figures predate the v19 uniform and school-facade corrections; see the redraw list.
