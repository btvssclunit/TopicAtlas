# 谈天说地 · Topic Atlas（示范版）

A free, no-login self-directed learning site for secondary Chinese Language, organised by topic. Produced by the BVSS MTL CL Unit, led by HOD/MTL Mdm Chun Kai Xin.

示范版: content is still being vetted. Not yet for student use.

- No sign-in, no analytics, no cookies. Your stream and level choice stays on your own device.
- All content is original writing by the BVSS CL Unit. Textbook texts are referred to by title and unit only.
- Practice chatbots and assignments are provided by Edcafe AI, a third-party service, and are labelled where they appear.

Live (示范版, noindex): https://btvssclunit.github.io/TopicAtlas/

Run locally: `python3 -m http.server`, then open http://localhost:8000.
